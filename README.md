A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

The hero arrives early — before the eclipse ritual on Thursday, before the
siege forms at noon — and the Dark Lord, too worn out to fight tonight,
plays host instead: tea gone cold twice, a chair by the fire, a blanket.
The duel is deferred to tomorrow, same hour, with bureaucratic precision.
Three hundred years of curses behind him, minions who unionised over tea
breaks, knees that creak louder than the tower gates — and a sting in the
last line: he is glad the hero came, because no one here listens. The hero
does.

The monologue is 99 words and 524 characters — inside the 95–100 word
target and the 600-character gate.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `/output` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
