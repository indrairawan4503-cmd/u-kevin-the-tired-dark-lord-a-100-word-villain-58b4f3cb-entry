You're early. The eclipse is Thursday; the siege, noon. Come in. Sit — that chair, by the fire. Tea? I made it at six. It's cold now. Twice cold. The kettle boiled three times while I waited. I lost count of the cups. My knees creak more than the tower gates. Three hundred years of curses, and the minions unionised over tea breaks. I signed. They were right. No duel tonight. Take the blanket. The spare one. Tomorrow, same hour, bring the sword. Rest a while. Please stay. I am glad you came. No one here listens. You do.
